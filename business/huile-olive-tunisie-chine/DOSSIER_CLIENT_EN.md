## Why Tunisian olive oil, and why now

- Tunisia is among the **world's top 3-4 olive oil exporters**, supplying the brands and bottlers of Spain, Italy and the US for decades — the same quality, one step closer to the source.
- China imported ≈ **40,000 tonnes** of olive oil in 2025 (**+54% year-on-year**). Spain holds ~82% of the market; Tunisia is only its ~8th supplier. **The origin is under-represented, not under-qualified** — first movers capture both margin and story.
